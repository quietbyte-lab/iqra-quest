How this page works — read me first

Each white box below = one sound the teacher taught. When he teaches a sound he usually repeats it several times, so a box contains several recordings of the same thing.

  1. Press ▶ Play all to hear the whole box, or press the numbered buttons ★ to hear each repetition on its own. The numbers are just repetition 1, 2, 3… — the same sound said again.
  2. Click the ★ on whichever repetition sounds clearest. That is the one that will go into the app. (It starts on the longest one.)
  3. Tell me what it is in the “This clip is…” row: a letter sound, a word, only useful for comparison, or not useful at all. Correct my guessed letter and vowel if needed.
  4. Add a comment if you want me to know how to use it.

To join clips together (e.g. to contrast س with ص): press the on any recordings you want — from any page — then use the bar at the bottom of the screen to preview and save them as one clip.

Your work saves automatically in this browser. Nothing goes into the app until you export and I publish it. You do not have to do all of them.
